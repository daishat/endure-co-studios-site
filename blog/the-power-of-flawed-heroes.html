The Power of Flawed Heroes

By D’Aisha · September 25, 2026

Do you know what every superhero has in common? Every single one of them makes mistakes. They show their strengths, of course, but they also show their flaws.

I believe writers create them this way to build a connection with readers. Perfect people don’t feel real, and they can seem impossible to live up to. Seeing these amazing people face their mistakes and make up for them is inspiring. They remind us that we can make mistakes and still be great.

It’s easy to get stuck on your mistakes and feel like they’re the only thing that matters, but that’s not the case. Just like superheroes learn from their mistakes and come out better, so can we.

I have made multiple mistakes that left me feeling like I wouldn’t be able to become the person I wanted to be. It took a lot of soul-searching to realize that I am not defined by those mistakes. I have been getting better slowly, while still trying to accomplish my goals. I learned to change the way I think. Instead of beating myself up when I mess up, I fix it and make sure I don’t do it again.

I want anyone who reads this to know: it’s not just you.

Sometimes, situations are out of your control, and you may feel lost. I think everyone goes through that phase in life. There are seasons when you feel directionless and wonder whether your hard work will ever pay off.

Sometimes, it feels like you’re making life-altering mistakes—like forgetting important documents for a big project before a promotion or not being able to afford the car parts you need to get to work. These moments can feel impossible to move past.

But remember, superheroes have devastating moments too, and they still stand up.

Those are the real superheroes: the ones who get knocked down by life and decide to get back up.

Stories remind us that growth doesn’t mean being perfect. It’s about choosing to keep going.
