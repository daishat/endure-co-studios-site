PERSONAL GROWTH & REFLECTION

Life Happened...but I'm back

By D’Aisha · July 15, 2026

Hello everyone,

It’s been a while since we last talked. I’m sorry I’ve been away, but life has been heavy lately. I know I've been gone, but let me tell you what I have been up to.

My daughter is getting ready to start school for the first time, and I’m honestly not sure how I feel about it. On one hand, with her going to school, I can finally concentrate better. As I get older, the term mom brain has become more and more real lately. I can barely remember what I have to do when I walk in a room. I have to make lists just to make sure my household is running smoothly.

On top of that, I am back in school for Medical Billing and Coding. I am trying to have a stable career in this crazy economy. Side note: the job market is horrible! I've applied for jobs I know I'm qualified for, only to receive rejection emails or sometimes no response at all. So my family has been doing that. With her going to school, it would let me work in the field again instead of only remote or night shift.

On the other hand, my child is my whole heart and I’m worried about her. We have never been apart from each other since she was born. She is going to be around so many people I don’t know. I have to trust these people to take care of my child. She is too young for a phone and I probably text her a lot.

I’m worried about something happening to her and she not being able to explain it to me. Or she is still hungry after lunch and wants more food. What if she just wants her mom? All these thoughts go through my mind constantly the closer we get to school time.

She grew up before I was ready and I don’t know how to handle it. The little girl who once needed me for everything is learning how to become independent. While I am happy for her, I’m sad and sometimes feel like she doesn’t need me anymore.

I have plenty of goals for our future and one of them is to be more consistent. I understand that success rarely happens overnight. It takes showing up constantly to grow.

It’s mentally hard to grow that discipline at this time. It feels like bills are closing in on us, our lives are flying by, and our dream life feels farther away. Sometimes, I stay up at night, unable to sleep due to the anxiety of trying to get to where I want to be.

I finally decided on something though. I am going to try to work on consistency. If I can be consistent, I think my anxiety would lighten up a bit. I'm not going to promise weekly posts, because I'd rather be honest than disappear again. I don't want to set myself up for failure. I will take it one step at a time.

If I can stay consistent, even in small ways, I know I'll eventually get where I want to be. So my goal is to make sure I have at least four posts a month. I am going to be more personal going forward. I’ll talk about my career path and the things I want to accomplish.

I hope we all learn to be more consistent for our futures.

Best,
Endure Co Founder
