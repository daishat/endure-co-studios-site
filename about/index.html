I’m D’Aisha!

The mind behind the studio.

Endure Co Studios is where the worlds in my imagination meet the life I’m living. I’m creating original stories, comics, games, and apps while navigating motherhood, work, and whatever life decides to throw at me next.

My mission is simple: build while you endure life. I’m building something that gives me the freedom to create, make room for my family, and shape a future of my own—even when life is messy.

I hope what I create gives you something, too: a world to get lost in, a laugh on a hard day, or a little help when your mind feels full.

Thank you for being here, exploring my work, and supporting the studio as it grows. Every read, play, purchase, and shared link helps me keep building.
