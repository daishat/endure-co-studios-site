The Aware

By D’Aisha · Original story and selected prose writing

Survival horror · Comic book in development

The scene

Robin encounters a mother and her infected daughter. The encounter forces her to imagine what the outbreak could mean for Adrian.

What this sample demonstrates

The contrast between the child’s speech and loss of control creates tension. Robin’s thoughts connect the horror to her relationship with her own daughter, while brief dialogue leaves room for the scene’s emotional weight.

This is a prose sample from a developing project, not a released comic, finished book, or playable game.
