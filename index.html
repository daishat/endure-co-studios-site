Meet D’Aisha

Hi, I'm D’Aisha, the creator behind Endure Co Studios.

I love turning everyday moments into stories, comics, apps, and creative projects.

I believe creativity helps people laugh, imagine, and endure life's difficult moments.

My goal is simple: create things that leave people feeling a little lighter than when they arrived.
