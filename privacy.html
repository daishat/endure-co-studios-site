Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

Who we are

Endure Co Studios is an independent storytelling and entertainment studio based in Tennessee. For privacy questions, contact founder@endurecostudios.com.

Contact inquiries

When you submit our contact form, your name, email address, and message are sent through FormSubmit to the studio’s contact inbox so we can respond. Please do not submit passwords, payment information, or sensitive personal information. We use validation, a hidden spam-trap field, and FormSubmit’s reCAPTCHA to reduce spam. Contact information is not included in our custom Google Analytics events.

Optional Google Analytics

When activated and you accept analytics, we use Google Analytics 4 to measure visits, approximate new and returning browsers, page popularity, referral sources, engagement, project-link clicks, and contact-form confirmations. Analytics uses pseudonymous browser identifiers and cookies. Google receives technical information such as device and browser information and processes network information as part of providing the service; analytics is not anonymous simply because names are excluded.

Our code does not send contact-form names, email addresses, or message text to Analytics. Custom click events use fixed project and action labels, not form values or complete destination URLs. Page URLs are stripped of fragments and query parameters except approved campaign-label fields. Referrers are reduced to their website origin. Do not put personal information in campaign labels or links you share.

Your analytics choice

Analytics is optional. Our Google Analytics tag is blocked until you accept, and advertising-related consent remains denied. Rejecting analytics does not prevent reading, shopping through our linked storefront, or contacting us. You can change your choice through Analytics settings in the footer. We remember that preference in this browser for up to 180 days. We honor the Global Privacy Control signal by keeping analytics off. Withdrawing consent stops future collection and clears accessible Google Analytics cookies; it does not automatically delete information already processed.

Storage and retention

We store your analytics preference locally in your browser. A temporary contact-confirmation token may be stored for the current tab, without your contact details, to avoid counting ordinary page visits as successful contact submissions. It expires for measurement after 30 minutes and is consumed once. Analytics cookies are configured for up to 180 days. The intended GA4 event-data retention setting is two months; aggregated reports may remain available longer. Contact-inquiry retention depends on the purpose of the inquiry and any applicable recordkeeping obligations. Contact us to request deletion of an inquiry.

Other services and external links

GitHub Pages hosts our site. FormSubmit and Google reCAPTCHA process contact submissions and spam checks. Google Fonts may receive network requests when fonts load. Ko-fi handles product checkout and delivery; Substack and our social channels operate under their own policies. These services may process information outside your state or country. Their practices are described in their policies: Google, FormSubmit, GitHub, Ko-fi, and Substack.

Privacy requests

Depending on where you live, you may have rights concerning your personal information, including access, correction, deletion, or withdrawal of consent. Send requests to the studio contact address above. We may need enough information to locate your inquiry and verify the request.
