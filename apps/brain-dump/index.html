Brain Dump

Too many thoughts. Nowhere to put them? Start here.

The idea you don’t want to lose. The to-do you keep remembering at the wrong time. The thought that won’t leave you alone. Brain Dump gives it all a place to land—without asking you to organize it first.

Capture what’s on your mind, then review AI-assisted suggestions to help sort out what comes next. You choose what to keep. Dump first. Organize second.

Preparing for testing and launch

Capture first. Find your next step.

Thought capture, saved entries, tasks, journaling, and AI-assisted sorting are being prepared for testing. AI suggestions are reviewed by you before you accept them.

Offline reliability, recycle-bin recovery, additional privacy controls, and premium options still need implementation or verification. They are not promised as launch-ready features.

Public access has not been announced.
